# Product Guidelines: Kron0

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Visual Style**: Neo-glassmorphism featuring translucent panels (`backdrop-blur`), subtle glowing borders, neon accents, and deep dark backgrounds.
- **Color Palette**: Dark baseline paired with vibrant neon accent gradients (cyan, purple, emerald, rose) for status indicators, active states, and time slot pills.
- **Typography**: Clean, high-legibility sans-serif typography optimized for dense schedule grids and digital timer displays.

## Tone & Voice
- **Tone**: Encouraging, crisp, modern, and student-focused. Avoid corporate jargon or intrusive microcopy.
- **User Feedback**: Energetic micro-rewards (e.g., celebration overlays, confetti on focus session completion) combined with concise toast notifications.

## UX & Interaction Principles
- **Fluid Micro-Animations**: Leverage Framer Motion for modal transitions, list staggering, tab switches, and interactive hover states.
- **Responsive Layout**: Ergonomic for both desktop and mobile viewports.
- **Zero-Friction State Changes**: Instant visual state updates paired with real-time Firestore sync and proactive error boundary handling.
- **PWA Excellence**: Fast startup, smooth offline status indicators, and full installability.
